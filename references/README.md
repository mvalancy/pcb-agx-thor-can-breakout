# Reference register

[Project](../README.md) · [Interface review](../docs/interface-and-protection.md) · [Connector audit](../research/connector-sourcing.md)

Original manufacturer PDFs and NVIDIA archives remain local under ignored `vendor/` or their original download location. They are not redistributed. `sources.json` records public URLs, document identifiers, the facts used, retrieval date and hashes of reviewed local copies where available. A hash identifies the reviewed bytes; it does not confer redistribution rights or verify a different carrier revision.
